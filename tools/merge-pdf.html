Merge PDF

Combine multiple PDF files into a single document.

What is Merge PDF?

Merge PDF is a free online tool that combines multiple PDF files into one document.

How to use it

  1. Click or drag your PDF files into the drop zone.
  2. Reorder by dragging.
  3. Click Merge PDFs.
  4. Your merged PDF downloads automatically.
