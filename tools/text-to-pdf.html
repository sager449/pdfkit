Text to PDF

Convert any text into a clean PDF document instantly.

What is Text to PDF?

Text to PDF is a free online tool that converts plain text into a PDF document.

How to use it

  1. Type or paste your text.
  2. Choose font size, alignment, and orientation.
  3. Click Generate PDF.
  4. Your PDF downloads instantly.
