Split PDF

Split a PDF into separate pages, or extract a range.

What is Split PDF?

Split PDF is a free online tool that divides a PDF into separate pages.

How to use it

  1. Upload a PDF file.
  2. Choose Split every page or Extract range.
  3. Click Split PDF.
  4. Download the resulting file(s).
